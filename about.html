Our Story

Founded in 2020 in the heart of Johannesburg, The Roast Cafe began with a simple yet ambitious vision: to bridge the gap between ethical coffee farming and the daily cups enjoyed by our local community. What started as a modest garage setup with one small-batch roaster has grown into a thriving artisanal roastery and cafe brand serving coffee lovers across Johannesburg.

Our journey is rooted in an unwavering passion for specialty coffee. We partner directly with sustainable, independent farmers across East Africa and South America to source 100% Arabica beans. By roasting every batch locally, we preserve distinct regional flavour profiles, from bright floral notes to rich, chocolatey undertones, making sure every bean reaches its peak before it meets the water.

Beyond serving exceptional espresso and artisanal filter coffees, The Roast Cafe is built around the idea of gathering. We believe a great cafe is the anchor of a neighbourhood: a place where commuters, creatives, and friends meet over fresh brews and comforting pastries.

Our Mission & Vision
